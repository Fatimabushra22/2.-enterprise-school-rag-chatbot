# STUDENT DRESS CODE AND UNIFORM POLICY

## 1. Purpose and General Guidelines
The school uniform promotes equality, discipline, and a sense of belonging among students. All students from Grades 1 to 12 are expected to arrive on campus in neat, clean, and properly pressed uniforms every working day.

## 2. Regular Academic Days (Monday to Thursday)
- **Boys (Grades 1–5):** Navy blue half-sleeve shorts, crisp white collared shirt with school monogram on the pocket, navy blue belt with school emblem, black velcro/lace leather shoes, and navy blue cotton socks.
- **Boys (Grades 6–12):** Navy blue formal trousers, crisp white full-sleeve or half-sleeve collared shirt, school striped tie, navy blue belt, black formal leather shoes, and navy blue socks.
- **Girls (Grades 1–5):** Navy blue pleated tunic, crisp white collared blouse, school tie, black buckle shoes, and navy blue socks.
- **Girls (Grades 6–12):** Navy blue divided skirt (knee-length) or formal trousers, crisp white collared shirt, school striped tie, black formal shoes, and navy blue socks.

## 3. Sports and House Activity Days (Friday and Saturday)
Students must wear their assigned House uniform based on their house allocation (Red House, Blue House, Green House, or Yellow House):
- **Top:** Breathable House-colored polo T-shirt with the school crest.
- **Bottom:** White track pants with dual navy side stripes.
- **Footwear:** Pure white canvas or running sports shoes with white socks.

## 4. Winter Uniform Guidelines (November 1 to February 28)
- **Grades 1–5:** Navy blue V-neck knitted sleeveless or full-sleeve sweater with the school crest.
- **Grades 6–12:** Navy blue tailored blazer with brass buttons and the official school crest is mandatory during morning assembly and formal school events. Navy blue woolen caps and mufflers are permitted during extreme cold weather.

## 5. Personal Grooming and Accessories
- **ID Card:** The laminated School Identity Card with the current academic year lanyard must be worn around the neck at all times on campus and on the school bus.
- **Hair & Nails:** Boys must maintain a short, neat haircut. Girls with shoulder-length or longer hair must tie it neatly into two braids or a ponytail using navy blue or black hairbands. Nails must be trimmed short and kept free of nail polish.
- **Prohibited Items:** Smartwatches, fitness bands, gold/expensive jewelry, mehendi/tattoos, and colored contact lenses are strictly prohibited. Only simple analog wristwatches are allowed from Grade 6 onward.

## 6. Non-Compliance Policy
- First violation: Written remark in the Student Almanac/Diary.
- Three consecutive violations: Parents will be notified by the Discipline Coordinator, and the student may not be permitted to attend regular classes until the uniform requirement is met.

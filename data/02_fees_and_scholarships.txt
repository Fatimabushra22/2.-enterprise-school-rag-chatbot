# ANNUAL FEE STRUCTURE, PAYMENT SCHEDULE, AND SCHOLARSHIPS

## 1. One-Time Admission Charges (New Students Only)
- **Registration & Prospectus Fee:** ₹1,000 (non-refundable, payable at the time of application).
- **Admission Fee:** ₹15,000 (one-time, non-refundable upon confirmation of seat).
- **Caution Money (Security Deposit):** ₹5,000 (refundable upon withdrawal from the school after clearing all library, laboratory, and transport dues).

## 2. Quarterly Tuition and Composite Fee Structure (2026–2027)
Fees are billed on a quarterly basis (4 quarters per academic year):
- **Primary Wing (Grades 1 to 5):** ₹13,500 per quarter (₹4,500/month).
- **Middle Wing (Grades 6 to 8):** ₹16,500 per quarter (₹5,500/month).
- **Secondary Wing (Grades 9 & 10):** ₹18,000 per quarter (₹6,000/month).
- **Senior Secondary Wing – Commerce & Humanities (Grades 11 & 12):** ₹20,400 per quarter (₹6,800/month).
- **Senior Secondary Wing – Science with Lab (Grades 11 & 12):** ₹22,500 per quarter (₹7,500/month).

## 3. Annual Activity and Technology Charges
Payable once per year along with Quarter 1 fees:
- **Smart Class, ERP Portal & Library Fee:** ₹4,500 per year.
- **Sports, Arts & Annual Event Fee:** ₹3,500 per year.

## 4. Quarterly Payment Deadlines and Late Penalties
- **Quarter 1 (April–June):** Due by April 10, 2026.
- **Quarter 2 (July–September):** Due by July 10, 2026.
- **Quarter 3 (October–December):** Due by October 10, 2026.
- **Quarter 4 (January–March):** Due by January 10, 2027.
- **Late Fee Penalty:** A grace period is provided until the 15th of the due month. From the 16th onward, a late fine of ₹50 per day is automatically charged by the ERP system. If fees remain unpaid for over 45 days, the student's name may be struck off the rolls.

## 5. Accepted Payment Modes
- Online via the School Parent Portal (UPI, Net Banking, Debit/Credit Cards) with zero convenience fee on UPI.
- Demand Draft (DD) or Account Payee Cheque drawn in favor of "Greenwood International School" submitted at the Accounts Counter (9:00 AM to 2:00 PM, Monday to Friday). Cash payments above ₹2,000 are not accepted.

## 6. Fee Concessions and Merit Scholarships
- **Sibling Concession:** A 15% discount on the quarterly tuition fee is granted to the younger sibling studying concurrently in the school.
- **Merit Scholarship (Grades 9–12):** Students scoring 95% and above in the annual school examination or CBSE Board examination receive a 50% tuition fee waiver for the following academic year.
- **Sports Excellence Waiver:** National-level medalists representing the school receive a 25% tuition fee concession upon approval by the Principal.
